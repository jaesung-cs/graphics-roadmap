## Attribution and changes

This is an adaptation of Robotics Roadmap (https://github.com/woolimi/robotics-roadmap), © 2026 Woolim Park, licensed under
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The changes made to it:

- The robotics lessons (ROS 2, kinematics and control, motion planning, grasping, state estimation, SLAM, navigation,
  autonomous driving, reinforcement learning, imitation learning, VLA and world models) were removed.
- In the kept lessons (vectors-matrices, derivatives, probability, linear-regression, perceptron, autograd, pytorch-mlp,
  cnn, resnet, vit, clip, tokenizer, transformer, gpt, vae, ddpm, flow-matching), links to removed lessons were turned into
  plain text.
- In all the kept lessons, the robotics examples were replaced with image and graphics examples and the robotics-only
  sections were removed; their text, figures and quizzes were rewritten accordingly, © 2026 jaesung-cs for the new
  material. In vae, the ACT section became a section on CVAE-based image-to-image translation; in ddpm, the Diffusion
  Policy section became a section on coarse-to-fine sampling; in flow-matching, the π0 and GR00T N1 sections became
  sections on the rectified flow of Stable Diffusion 3.
- The lessons of the `stable-diffusion` and `video` branches are new, © 2026 jaesung-cs, and released under the same
  license.
- `lessons/branches.yaml` was rewritten for the new curriculum map, a single tree that grows down from the first lesson
  instead of trees around a central hub, and the images of the robotics map in `docs/` were removed.
